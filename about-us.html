Our story

Station India serves honest Indian vegetarian food at a fair price, from breakfast dosas to late-evening paneer. Our kitchen is 100% vegetarian, with Jain options on request. The name comes from the Indian railway station: a place everyone passes through hungry and leaves satisfied.

What we stand for

Pure vegetarian

No meat, fish or egg in the kitchen.

Fresh and fast

Dosas, rotis and chaat made to order.

Fair prices

Most dishes sit between AED 1 and 50.
